AWS Ecosystem

You.com ARI Agents

Forensic Litigation OCR & Evidence Swarms

System Architecture for Thai Legal Document Discovery

The litigation evidence stack must handle massive volumes of image-based PDFs with variable quality: decades-old scanned records, stamped annotations, handwritten marginalia, and complex layouts combining Thai script, English loanwords, and governmental notation.

Thai-Script Specialization

Typhoon OCR emerges as the leading solution, developed by SCB 10X and the AI Research Institute of Thailand specifically for Thai documents. Achieves state-of-the-art results with end-to-end document understanding architecture.

Benchmark data from Thailand's National Anti-Corruption Commission (NACC) shows Tesseract limitations: 72% word-level accuracy on printed text, degrading to 59% on handwritten submissions [122] [123]. Their hybrid approach combining Tesseract + Gemini achieves 78.6% processing time reduction but is optimized for throughput, not litigation-grade accuracy.

Zero-Hallucination Guarantee Mechanisms

RAG Architecture

Vector indexing with Thai-optimized embeddings (WangchanBERTa), source attribution to page coordinates, and verbatim Thai quotation

Human-in-the-Loop

Confidence scoring (<0.85 → automatic review), flagged review queue, adjudication workflow with digital signatures

Chain-of-Custody

Immutable Merkle-tree logging, timestamped operations, HSM-protected digital signatures, exportable for court submission

Cloud-Based Deployment Model

Primary deployment leverages AWS/Azure/GCP managed OCR + LLM inference with data residency options for Thai PDPA compliance. Hybrid model combines cloud OCR with on-premises review for sensitive documents. Sovereign cloud options include True IDC, CAT Telecom, and AIS Cloud for domestic processing.
